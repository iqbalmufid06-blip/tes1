How it works

Live in a week, not a quarter.

No rip-and-replace. Nimbus runs on the trackers you already have, and your team keeps working the way it works today.

STEP 01

Connect your vehicles

Plug in your existing GPS trackers or hand drivers the Nimbus app. We support the hardware most SEA fleets already run.

STEP 02

Assign the day's jobs

Import your delivery list or create jobs by hand. Nimbus sequences the stops and pushes them to each driver.

STEP 03

Track it live

Watch the map, handle the exceptions Nimbus flags for you, and export the day's on-time report before you leave.
